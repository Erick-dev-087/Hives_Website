# Hives Pastries --- Website Design Specification

## Project Direction

A modern, energetic, food-first website for Hives Pastries. The site
should use subtle, purposeful interaction to tell the Hives story
without allowing animation to overshadow the products, information, or
ordering experience.

> **Core principle:** The website should make people want the food
> first, then make it easy to get the food.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Website Structure

The site is a **one-page homepage with supporting pages**, rather than
forcing the entire catalogue into one long page.

### Homepage

The homepage runs from:

**Hero → About → Delivery → Product Range → Interactive "Made for the
Moment" Journey → Social Proof → CTA → Footer**

The homepage exists primarily to:

-   Introduce Hives.
-   Create desire.
-   Communicate the brand.
-   Show the breadth of what Hives offers.
-   Build trust.
-   Lead customers toward ordering.

The homepage should **not** contain the entire product catalogue.

### Menu Page

The full catalogue lives on a separate **Menu** page.

Customers should be able to browse:

-   Cakes
-   Cupcakes
-   Ice Cream & Sundaes
-   Smoothies
-   Milkshakes
-   Yoghurt
-   Coffee
-   Mocktails
-   Pastries
-   Loaves / Bakes
-   Breakfast Combos
-   Other confirmed Hives products

The final categories must follow the real Hives catalogue.

Menu functionality should include:

-   Category filtering.
-   Search where useful.
-   Product cards.
-   Product images.
-   Prices.
-   Product details.
-   Craving/occasion discovery.
-   Clear ordering actions.

### Product Detail Page / View

Products with meaningful options should have a dedicated product-detail
experience.

The distinction is:

> **Homepage = inspire.**\
> **Menu = help customers discover and choose.**\
> **Product page = help customers configure and order.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Ordering

Hives will use **WhatsApp as the primary ordering channel**.

The website should prepare the customer's order and send the relevant
details to WhatsApp.

### Intended flow

**Browse → Select product → Choose options → Add to order → Review order
→ Send to WhatsApp**

The WhatsApp message should contain, where applicable:

-   Product name
-   Quantity
-   Size/weight
-   Flavour
-   Cake message
-   Toppings/extras
-   Other selected options
-   Estimated total

The first version does not need a large e-commerce system with accounts,
inventory management, or complicated checkout.

The goal is to make ordering through WhatsApp much easier and more
structured.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Brand & Visual Direction

## Theme

**Primary theme: Light.**

The site should use a warm, clean background. Food photography should
remain the visual hero.

## Colour Palette

The existing Hives logo is the source of the visual identity. The
website should remain consistent with it.

### Core colours

-   **Honey Yellow:** `#FFDF18`
-   **Hive Black:** `#111111`
-   **Warm Cream / White:** `#FFFDF7`

### Supporting colours

-   **Soft Vanilla:** `#FFF7D6`
-   **Honey Gold:** `#F5B800`
-   **Cocoa Brown:** `#4A2C20`
-   **Warm Gray:** `#6B6658`

Supporting colours are accents only. The core identity remains
**yellow + black + warm light backgrounds**.

## Typography

Use a modern **sans-serif** system.

The typography should feel clean, friendly, contemporary, readable, and
energetic without becoming childish.

Possible directions include:

-   Plus Jakarta Sans
-   Manrope
-   Sora
-   Inter
-   DM Sans

The final choice can be made during implementation based on visual
testing and performance.

> Hives to you → discover the variety → experience what Hives can create
> for life's moments → explore the full menu → order.**

The entire homepage does not need to be one continuous animation. The
story is created by the sequence of sections, while selected sections
provide the memorable interactive moments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Signature Interactive Section --- "Made for the Moment"

This is the major visual experience.

It is not another product catalogue. It communicates:

> **Hives creates treats for moments worth celebrating.**

### Concept

A bee moves from one featured product to another.

A realistic cake or food item sits on a realistic plate/tray.

The bee approaches.

The plate subtly rotates.

A trail of honey or flour can guide the bee toward the next product.

The next product appears.

The journey continues.

### Example sequence

**Birthday Cake** - Bee arrives. - Plate subtly rotates. - Short message
about birthdays.

**Graduation Cake** - Bee leaves. - Flour/honey trail transitions to the
next plate. - Graduation cake appears.

**Themed Cake** - Bee moves to the next cake. - Themed design becomes
the focus.

**Cupcakes** - Plate interaction continues or adapts.

**Drinks / Smoothies** - Use a glass or tray rather than forcing
everything onto a plate.

**Ice Cream** - Bee interacts subtly with the bowl/cone.

### Restraint

Use approximately **4--6 strong moments**, not the entire catalogue.

This is the signature brand/storytelling experience.

The full catalogue remains on the Menu page.

# 15. Interaction Philosophy

The website should contain interaction throughout, but interaction must
remain **subtle and purposeful**.

## Core rule

> **Every interaction should either tell the story, help the user
> navigate, provide feedback, or make the product more desirable.**

### Hero

-   Subtle food movement
-   Bee entrance
-   CTA hover

### About

-   Gentle image/text reveal

### Delivery

-   Small bee/package/destination interaction

### Product range

-   Responsive cards
-   Image movement
-   Hover states

### Signature experience

-   Strongest animation
-   Bee movement
-   Rotating plates/trays
-   Realistic product transitions
-   Honey/flour trails

### Menu

-   Filtering
-   Search
-   Category transitions
-   Hover states

### Product page

-   Option selection
-   Dynamic price changes
-   Quantity
-   Order summary
-   WhatsApp preparation

### Reviews

-   Subtle carousel/scroll if useful

### CTA

-   Small micro-interactions

# 16. Animation Philosophy --- Organic Motion

Motion should feel physical rather than technological.

Preferred characteristics:

-   Gentle acceleration
-   Gentle deceleration
-   Natural pauses
-   Subtle rotation
-   Slight overshoot
-   Realistic movement
-   Controlled timing

The bee should feel like a bee:

> **buzz → approach → slow down → hover → land → pause → leave**

It should not feel like an icon being moved around purely to demonstrate
CSS/JavaScript.

Likewise:

-   Plates should have weight.
-   Food should feel grounded.
-   Honey should feel viscous.
-   Flour should feel soft.
-   Images should have depth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. The Bee

The bee is a **brand guide**, not a permanent attention-grabbing mascot.

It can:

-   Enter the hero.
-   Guide the signature interaction.
-   Appear in delivery.
-   Connect selected transitions.
-   Return near the final CTA.

It should not constantly fly around the screen.

The site must remain completely usable if the visitor ignores the bee.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. Mobile Behaviour

Do not simply shrink desktop animation.

The mobile experience should be designed around vertical scrolling.

The signature journey can become:

**Bee ↓ Cake ↓ Graduation Cake ↓ Themed Cake ↓ Cupcake ↓ Drink ↓ Ice
Cream**

Animations must remain lightweight and must never prevent access to
content or ordering.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19. Assets & Media Organization

**Real Hives Pastries assets are a major part of the design.**

The website should prioritize actual Hives photography over generic
stock photography.

### Media directory

Assets will be stored in a dedicated **media file/folder structure**.

The assets should ideally be categorized into folders for easier
development.

Suggested structure:

``` text
media/
├── cakes/
│   ├── birthday/
│   ├── graduation/
│   ├── anniversary/
│   ├── themed/
│   └── other/
├── cupcakes/
├── smoothies/
├── milkshakes/
├── yoghurt/
├── ice-cream/
├── pastries/
├── coffee/
├── mocktails/
├── breakfast/
├── brand/
│   ├── logo/
│   └── other-brand-assets/
├── people/
└── general/
```

The exact structure can change as more assets are collected.

### Asset requirement

Before implementing complex food animations, collect enough good-quality
real product images.

For the signature interactive section, images should ideally be:

-   High resolution
-   Well lit
-   Visually consistent
-   Easy to isolate where necessary
-   Photographed from useful angles
-   Suitable for the intended background/composition

Do not build the entire interaction around placeholders and discover
later that the actual Hives photos cannot support it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. Performance & UX

The site should remain fast despite its visual ambition.

Avoid:

-   Excessive scroll listeners
-   Huge unoptimized images
-   Unnecessary video backgrounds
-   Animation on every element
-   Long loading sequences
-   Interactions that block navigation
-   Excessive JavaScript for simple effects

Prefer:

-   Optimized images
-   Lazy loading
-   CSS transforms where appropriate
-   Efficient viewport observation
-   Lightweight animation tooling only where justified
-   Reduced-motion support
-   Progressive enhancement

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. Accessibility

Animations must never be required to understand the website.

The site must remain usable if:

-   Animations are disabled.
-   JavaScript fails.
-   The user prefers reduced motion.
-   The user navigates by keyboard.
-   The user is on a small screen.

Important information must exist as real semantic content, not only
inside animation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 22. Conversion Philosophy

This is a **business website**, not an interactive art project.

Every major visual experience should ultimately lead toward:

1.  **Explore**
2.  **Choose**
3.  **Order**

The desired journey is:

> **See something delicious → become curious → discover options → choose
> something → configure it if necessary → order through WhatsApp.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 23. Final Design Direction

Hives Pastries should feel like:

> **A modern digital hive where the food is the star and the bee quietly
> guides the journey.**

The website combines:

-   Hives' existing yellow/black identity
-   Warm light backgrounds
-   Modern sans-serif typography
-   Real food photography
-   Subtle honey/flour visual language
-   Organic motion
-   A memorable bee-led interactive sequence
-   A practical Menu
-   Useful product configuration
-   WhatsApp ordering

The result should be:

**Energetic without being chaotic.**\
**Interactive without being confusing.**\
**Beautiful without becoming a portfolio piece.**\
**Functional enough to generate real orders.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

development:

-   Light theme
-   Hives brand colour palette
-   Modern sans-serif typography direction
-   One-page homepage
-   Separate Menu page
-   Product detail experience where needed
-   WhatsApp ordering

-   Cravings/occasion discovery within the Menu
-   Real Hives product photography
-   Bee-led signature interaction
-   Rotating plates/trays for selected products
-   Honey/flour transition trails
-   Subtle purposeful interactions throughout
-   Mobile-specific interaction behaviour
-   Performance and accessibility requirements

### Remaining information to verify during implementation

Only business-data questions should remain open:

-   Final product catalogue
-   Exact product names
-   Exact prices
-   Exact flavours
-   Exact sizes/weights
-   Exact customization options
-   Delivery boundaries and fees
-   Opening hours
-   Final contact details
-   Final social links
-   Product photography/assets

These are **content/data collection tasks**, not reasons to delay
development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Guiding Principle

> **Do not keep designing forever. Build the first version.**

The visual system and interaction philosophy are sufficiently defined to
begin development. Refinements can be made once real content, assets,
and a working interface are visible.
